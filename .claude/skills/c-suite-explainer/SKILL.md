# Explaining results to King and a C-suite reader

Follow `docs/playbook/working_with_king.md` and use only the terms in `docs/playbook/glossary.md`.

1. **Lead with the answer** in plain words. No term the reader has not met yet.
2. **Define before use**, through one running example (the 62-year-old woman, her record, her groups).
3. **Use the agreed terms only**, the same way every time. Compare every method with the survey alone.
4. **Numbers come with their meaning:** what it is, what it means (the page's scale: 1.5-1.7 best possible,
   3 good enough to act on, 5 the rough level only, 15 guessing blind), and so what for the decision. Say
   "median" and explain it in plain words where first used; link every number to where it is shown. Explain how accuracy is measured before the
   first result, and say each finding once; later mentions link back to it rather than repeating it.
5. **Layer the page.** A short main path of five numbered sections on colour bands (answer, two kinds, what to
   do as a decision tree, how we know in the answer's order, limits and what to test next) with detail
   folded one click away; test it on a cold reader before sharing (`qa_checklist.md`).
6. **One idea per paragraph.** Never introduce a second concept while explaining the first. No notes left
   floating away from what they explain.
7. **Claims are checked.** If asked "is this backed up?", recompute from the data and say where it comes from.
   Say "about" unless numbers are equal. Do not claim untested capabilities.
8. **When King drafts wording**, keep the intent, fix grammar, and say exactly what changed and why.
9. **When King says "discuss with me"**, give a view with options and wait for a decision before editing.
10. **Recap changes** by quoting the new wording.
11. **One integrated pass, not patches.** When King has several comments, collect them all, then change
    the whole page against the agreed principles (the answer is the spine; parallel things alike; explain where
    first met; one idea per sentence; say which kind of AI and whether blended; visual consistency) and list
    every change with its new wording before publishing. Keep the method's inner workings (fitted weights,
    worked sums) for the analysts' section, and show design choices as side-by-side mock-ups.
12. **QA before sharing** with `docs/playbook/qa_checklist.md` (accuracy, language, accessibility, aesthetics).
